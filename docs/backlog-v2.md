## Shifts Domain

### Manual bartender assignment by manager
**Origin:** Requested during v.1 planning (2026-03-21). Scoped down to removal-only for v.1.
**Description:** A manager can assign a bartender to a shift directly, without requiring the bartender to sign up themselves.
**Why deferred:** Raises consent and notification questions not addressed in v.1. v.1 supports only manager-initiated removal of bartenders who have already registered.
**Related v.1 items:** REQ-SHF-016, TSK-SHF-016, TSK-SHF-017, US-011 AF-5
